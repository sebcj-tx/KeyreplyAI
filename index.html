FAQ

Will it sound like a robot?

No. Messages are written in your tone, use the lead's name and property, and hand off to you when a human is needed.

Which lead sources work?

Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook/Google ads, website forms and phone calls — anything that can send an email or webhook.

Is texting leads legal?

Yes when the lead has contacted you or given consent. We register your number for A2P 10DLC, honor STOP instantly, and keep consent records.
